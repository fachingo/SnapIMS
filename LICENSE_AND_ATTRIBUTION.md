## Wikipedia and MediaWiki data

English Wikipedia pages are generally available under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike terms, subject to page-specific notices and third-party content. MediaWiki API software/documentation and Wikipedia trademarks have separate terms. SLMC stores page identity, revision, retrieval time and attribution metadata so downstream use can identify the source.

Structured factual statements may not themselves be protected expression, while copied summaries/extracts can be. Do not assume that storing or redistributing prose is obligation-free. Preserve attribution and obtain legal advice for commercial redistribution where needed.

## Required operating attribution record

For each sourced Movie, retain:

- provider: English Wikipedia;
- page title and page ID;
- source URL;
- source revision ID;
- retrieval timestamp;
- parser version;
- raw-response hash;
- attribution text/licence notice.

## Prohibited use

Do not automatically place Wikipedia prose in Shopify descriptions. Do not store commercial poster artwork without rights. Do not imply endorsement by Wikimedia Foundation.
